Sounds of Raintile

The .wav files in this folder are the "UI Audio" set (UI SFX Set) by Kenney
Vleugels (www.kenney.nl), converted from Ogg Vorbis to 16-bit mono WAV at
the same peak level. The set is released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0):
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
"You may use these assets in personal and commercial projects. Credit
(Kenney or www.kenney.nl) would be nice but is not mandatory."

Your own sounds go in Sounds\ of your data folder (%APPDATA%\Rainmeter\Raintile),
never here: an update of Raintile replaces this folder.
